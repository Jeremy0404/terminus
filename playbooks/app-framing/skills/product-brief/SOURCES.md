- Shape Up, chapter "Write the Pitch": the five ingredients of a pitch, namely problem ("the raw idea, a use case, or something we've seen that motivates us to work on this"), appetite, solution, rabbit holes ("details about the solution worth calling out to avoid problems") and no-gos, which give the brief its sections: https://basecamp.com/shapeup/1.5-chapter-06
- Claude Code best practices: the most useful specs are self-contained and end with a verification that proves the feature works (the "Done when" section): https://code.claude.com/docs/en/best-practices
- Anthropic, "Effective context engineering for AI agents": the smallest set of high-signal tokens (the brief rides along in every run of the app, so it stays short): https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents
